## Body

Old glue. Dried adhesive. Residue from wherever those nails have been.

Sticky tabs are single-use by design. The moment they touch a nail and then a nail bed, they've done their job. Reusing them means weaker adhesion, an uneven surface, and nails that lift before noon.

We made a different decision with The Daily French.

This is not about being wasteful. It's about having a real standard for what goes on your nails every day.

A fresh start should actually be fresh.
