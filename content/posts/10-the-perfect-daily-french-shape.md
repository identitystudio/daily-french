## Body

Most press-on nail shapes are designed for photos.

Too long to type in. Too square to feel natural. Too stark to look modern.

We designed the Daily French shape for real life.

**Medium-short softly rounded almond.**
Long enough to look intentional. Short enough to type, text, cook, clean, and live a full day without a single thought about your nails.

**Slightly shorter thumb.**
Most nail sets ignore the thumb. We didn't. The thumb is proportionally shorter than the fingers — a small detail that makes an enormous difference in how natural the set feels on real hands.

**Matte milky base.**
Not a harsh stark white. Not sheer. A soft opaque milky white with a matte finish — modern, understated, finished.

**Softly glossy French tip.**
The French tip carries a gentle gloss that contrasts the matte base without shouting. Classic. Clean. Softened.

Every decision was made for a woman who lives in her hands and wants a fresh French manicure every morning — not a manicure she has to protect.
