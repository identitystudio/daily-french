# Post 06 — How to apply The Daily French in 5 minutes

**Format:** TikTok tutorial / Instagram Reel / Step-by-step blog
**Required phrase:** clean, finished hands

---

## Hook

Five minutes. That's all it takes to go from bare to clean, finished hands with The Daily French.

---

## Body

Here is the exact morning routine:

**Step 1 — Prep (1 min)**
Push back your cuticles. Buff lightly if needed. Wipe each nail with the prep pad included in your set. No oil. No lotion. Dry nails only.

**Step 2 — Size (1 min)**
Lay out the 10 nails from your daily set. Match each one to your nail. The fit should cover the nail without touching the skin at the sides.

**Step 3 — Tab (1 min)**
Apply the adhesive tab to your nail, not to the press-on. Press firmly from the base, working outward. No air bubbles.

**Step 4 — Press (1 min)**
Align the press-on from the cuticle line down. Press firmly for 10–15 seconds on each nail. Squeeze the sides in slightly.

**Step 5 — Finish (1 min)**
File any edges if needed. That's it.

You now have clean, finished hands. Before the coffee is even done.

At night: warm soapy water, 60 seconds, done. No acetone. No scraping. The set slides off cleanly.
