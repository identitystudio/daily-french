## Body

It's for the woman who uses her hands all day.

The one who types the proposals, makes the meals, opens the packages, wipes the counters, holds the coffee cup, the laundry basket, the laptop, the child.

She wants clean, finished hands. But she doesn't want to spend two weeks protecting a manicure or apologizing for chips.

She's the woman who washes her hands properly. Who thinks about what's sitting under a lifted press-on. Who understands that clean hands and beautiful hands don't have to be separate things.

She's done with:
- Old glue sitting around
- Reused sticky tabs that lost their grip
- Nails that look tired by day six
- Choosing between practical and polished

The Daily French is her answer. One fresh set every morning. Applied in minutes. Worn all day. Removed at night. A fresh start tomorrow.

If that sounds like you — this is for you.
