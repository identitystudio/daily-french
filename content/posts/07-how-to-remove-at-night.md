## Body

This is the part people don't expect to be easy.

**The removal:**

Fill a small bowl with warm soapy water. Soak your nails for 30–60 seconds. The tabs are designed to release cleanly in warm water — you won't need to pry or force anything.

Gently push from the side of each nail. They slide off.

Wipe your nail bed with a clean cloth. Done.

No acetone. No scraping. No residue. No damaged natural nails. The whole thing takes about 60 seconds.

That's the point of The Daily French. You wear the day. You toss the day. You start fresh tomorrow.

The set you wore today has done its job. Tomorrow morning, you open a new one. A fresh individually sealed set, untouched, ready for the day ahead.

No old glue. No reused tabs. Nothing carries over.
